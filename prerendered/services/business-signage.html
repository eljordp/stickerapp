Signage FAQ

Business signage questions

Do you make business signs in Hayward?

Yes. The Sticker Smith makes custom business signs and signage in Hayward for storefronts, offices, restaurants, service companies, pop-ups, and retail locations across the East Bay.

Can you install storefront signs and window graphics?

Yes. Professional Bay Area installation is available for storefront signs, window graphics, wall graphics, vinyl lettering, acrylic signs, and A-frame signage.

What should I send for a signage quote?

Send photos of the install area, rough dimensions, your logo or artwork, the business location, and whether you need design help, printing only, or full installation.
